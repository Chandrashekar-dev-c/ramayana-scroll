RAMAYANA SCROLL — TATAKA LEVEL: EVERYTHING TO GENERATE
For Gemini. 45 images in 7 groups. Generate in this order; each group builds on the one before.

HOW TO USE (read first)
- Attach tataka-frame.png (the reference painting) to every image.
- Groups 2 and 3: also attach that character's approved sheet from Group 1.
- Save each image with the exact file name given. The game finds images by these names.
- Use each prompt as written, then add the STYLE block below at the end (except the story panels, which have their own).
- Make 2-4 candidates of each and keep the best. Reject: wrong finger count, figure not facing right, a figure turned to the viewer, cropped figure, background not flat magenta, a held object drawn twice.
- Pose strips: all poses of one character in one image, side by side, the SAME scale, with wide empty magenta gaps between them so they can be cut apart. Feet of standing poses on one invisible line.

STYLE BLOCK (paste after every prompt in Groups 1-3, 5-7)
STYLE: follow the attached reference painting for drawing style, proportions, ornament and colour. Traditional Mithila (Madhubani) painting from Bihar, in the manner of old village wall paintings, hand-painted with natural pigments: lampblack, indigo, turmeric yellow, saffron, madder red, leaf green, ochre and terracotta. Thick black double outlines on every shape with a thin coloured band between the two lines. Clean, even pigment fills (the painted grain is added later by the game, so keep surfaces clean), with dense fine line-hatching, dots, small triangles and lotus or fish motifs covering every garment area. No smooth shading, no gradients, no glow, no 3D rendering, not a cartoon. Figures with body, head and feet in strict side profile, never turned toward the viewer, one large almond-shaped eye, stylised elongated bodies, ornamented clothing. Reverent, dignified, storybook clarity. No text, no letters, no signature, no watermark.

AVOID (for tools that take a negative prompt)
thin uniform lines, paper texture, grain, noise, photorealistic, 3D render, anime, cartoon, film still, modern digital art, gradient shading, glow, lens flare, blurry, extra fingers, extra limbs, fused hands, deformed face, both eyes visible in profile, text, letters, watermark, signature, cropped figure, cast shadow on background, floor, ground line, scenery, front-facing face, duplicated weapon

BACKGROUND RULE (Groups 1-3, 5-7)
Background: one flat, perfectly uniform solid magenta (#FF00FF) colour filling the whole image, no texture, no floor, no shadow, nothing else.
